# Research Questions: Post-Launch Impact Review

## Goal
Validate whether a launched feature delivered the intended outcome — and surface unintended effects, use cases you didn't design for, and friction that's suppressing adoption.

## Screener criteria
- Used the feature at least once in the 4 weeks after launch
- Include two groups: users who kept using it after the first try, and users who tried it and stopped (both are informative; don't just talk to the enthusiasts)
- Exclude users who used the feature only because of a prompted notification or email — you want self-motivated use
- Confirm they can recall their experience with the feature specifically, not just the product generally

## Opening (5 min)

1. How do you use [product] generally — what's your main use case?
2. Have you used [feature name] since it launched? Roughly how often?
3. Before [feature] existed, how were you handling [the thing the feature was designed for]?

*Purpose: establish the before-state so you can compare it to the after-state.*

## Core questions (25–30 min)

### Intended impact
4. Walk me through the last time you used [feature]. What were you trying to do?

   *Probe: Is that what you were using it for when you first tried it, or has your use case shifted?*

5. Before [feature] launched, how would you have done that same thing?

   *Probe: How does that compare — is [feature] meaningfully faster, easier, or better? Or roughly the same?*

6. Has [feature] changed anything about how you use [product] overall?

   *Probe: Are there things you do more of, or things you've stopped doing, since [feature] launched?*

### Unintended effects
7. Has [feature] created any problems or complications for you that didn't exist before?

   *Probe: Was that something you expected, or a surprise?*

8. Has anyone else in your team or workflow been affected by [feature] — in ways they might not have anticipated?

   *Probe: Positive or negative?*

9. Is there anything [feature] does that you use it for that you don't think was the intended purpose?

   *Probe: How did you discover that use case?*

### Why adoption did or didn't stick
10. [For users who kept using it] What made you keep using it after the first try?

    *Probe: Was it immediately useful, or did it take a few uses to click?*

    [For users who tried and stopped] What made you stop after the first try?

    *Probe: Was it not useful for your situation, confusing, or just not the right time?*

11. Is there anything about [feature] that's slightly off — something that works but not quite the way you want it to?

    *Probe: Have you found a workaround, or do you just live with it?*

### Outcome validation
12. When you think about what [feature] was probably built to help with — do you feel like it accomplished that for you?

    *Probe: What specifically did it accomplish, or fail to accomplish?*

13. If [feature] disappeared tomorrow, would you notice?

    *Probe: What would you lose? What would you do instead?*

### Future direction
14. Is there something [feature] currently doesn't do that would make it significantly more useful for you?

    *Probe: How often does that gap come up?*

15. Is there a related problem — something adjacent to what [feature] does — that you still don't have a good solution for?

    *Probe: Is it related enough that you'd expect [product] to address it, or is it out of scope?*

## Closing (5 min)

16. If you were writing the review of [feature] for a colleague who was deciding whether to use [product] — what would you say about it?

17. Is there anything about your experience with [feature] — positive or negative — that you think the team should know?

## What to code for
- **Before vs. after behavior:** Did the feature actually change how they do the task, or are they using the feature alongside their old approach?
- **Unintended use cases:** Ways users are using the feature that weren't anticipated — these are often the most interesting expansion opportunities
- **Adoption drop-off reasons:** For users who tried and stopped — is it a fit issue, a quality issue, or a timing issue?
- **Adjacent unmet needs:** Problems that emerged now that this feature exists — what does the next feature need to be?
- **Outcome validation:** Did the feature deliver the outcome it was designed for? If users can't articulate a before-vs-after difference, the feature may be hitting the wrong problem

## Common mistake with this guide
Running the post-launch review only with users who loved the feature. The most valuable interviews are with users who tried it and stopped — they represent the gap between intent and execution. Talking only to enthusiasts will produce a glowing review that explains retention among early adopters but tells you nothing about why the feature isn't spreading. Always recruit a split between continuing users and lapsed users.
