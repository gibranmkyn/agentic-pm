# Metrics: B2C Social / Content Creation App — Weekly Active Creators

## North Star Metric
**Weekly Active Creators** — Viewers are abundant and cheap to acquire; creators are the scarce resource that makes the product worth anything. This metric proxies the supply-side health of the platform. When WAC grows, content supply grows, which drives viewer engagement, which attracts more creators. It's the flywheel entry point.

Core action: **Publishing content** — Publishing (not just drafting, not just logging in) is the repeatable behavior that produces value for all other users. Creators who publish weekly churn at a fraction of the rate of passive consumers.

## Metric tree

Weekly Active Creators
├── Acquisition
│   ├── New creator signups per week
│   └── Creator invite rate (existing creators inviting peers)
├── Activation
│   ├── % of new signups who publish within 7 days
│   └── Time-to-first-publish (median, new cohort)
└── Retention
    ├── Week-4 creator retention (% still publishing in week 4)
    ├── Publish frequency per active creator
    └── Creator resurrection rate (lapsed creators who restart)

## Guardrail metrics
- Viewer session length — guards against content quality degradation as creator volume grows
- Report / flag rate per piece of content — guards against spam or low-quality publishing inflating WAC

## What to watch at each stage
- **Pre-PMF:** Are any creators publishing more than once without being prompted? Unprompted repeat behavior before PMF is the signal that matters more than any volume metric.
- **Growth:** Week-4 creator retention. If creators are excited to sign up but stop publishing after the first week, the publishing experience or feedback loop is broken — not the acquisition funnel.
- **Mature:** Creator resurrection rate. At scale, reactivating lapsed creators is cheaper than acquiring new ones. A 5% resurrection rate is often worth more than a 5% acquisition lift.

## Common mistake for this product type
Optimizing for viewer engagement (watch time, session length) before creator retention is healthy. Viewer metrics are easy to move with algorithmic tweaks. They'll look good for one quarter and then crater when creators churn because they're not getting feedback loops that make publishing feel worthwhile. Creator health leads viewer health — not the other way around.
